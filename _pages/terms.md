This is a static personal website. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, serves no advertising,
and has no comment system, contact form, or mailing list. There is nothing here that collects
information about you.

The site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). GitHub records standard server
logs for the requests it serves, which is outside my control. Their handling of that data is
described in the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

Some pages embed video from YouTube or link out to other sites. Those third parties have their own
privacy practices, which apply once you play an embedded video or follow a link.

If you email me, I will use your address to reply to you and nothing else. I will not share it.

Questions about any of this are welcome at
[ben.dichter@catalystneuro.com](mailto:ben.dichter@catalystneuro.com).
